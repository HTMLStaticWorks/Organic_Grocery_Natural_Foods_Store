Common Questions

Subscription FAQ

Have questions about delivery, organic standards, or how to pause? Read below.

When will my organic box arrive?

+

We deliver boxes every Tuesday and Friday morning between 7:00 AM and 11:00 AM to preserve cool temperature of fresh crops. You will receive an SMS tracking link once our driver departs.

Can I customize the vegetables in my box?

+

Yes, absolutely! Subscribed members receive an email every Sunday showing the upcoming harvest list. You can log in and swap out any items you do not want with alternatives.

How do I pause or cancel my subscription?

+

You can pause or cancel at any time directly through your user dashboard with no cancellation fees or lock-in contracts. Make sure to update your schedule before Monday noon.
